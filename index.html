Conference Overview

The International Conference on Translational Research (ICTR) is a flagship global event organized by the Indian Society for Translational Research (ISTR), dedicated to bridging the gap between fundamental discoveries and clinical or industrial applications. The 8th ICTR 2025, themed “Bridging Discoveries to Solutions: Advancing Translational Research,” continues this legacy by addressing emerging challenges in disease biology, therapeutics, artificial intelligence, and biotechnology.

Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology (KIIT) Deemed to be University, Bhubaneswar, is one of India’s most dynamic and research-intensive universities, known for academic excellence and innovation. Founded by Prof. (Dr.) Achyuta Samanta, the university has hosted numerous international symposia and continues to serve as a hub for scientific dialogue, fostering translational and socially impactful research.
